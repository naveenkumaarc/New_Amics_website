About us

Engineering that keeps production running

Amics Automations is an industrial automation and engineering solutions company specialising in the design, development, manufacturing and integration of customised automation systems for production and assembly industries. We deliver turnkey solutions covering mechanical design, electrical engineering, control panels, PLC and HMI programming, machine software, system integration, installation, commissioning and after-sales support.

Our focus is on reliable, practical, production-oriented systems that improve productivity, process consistency, traceability, product quality and operator safety.

Our operating principle
An automation system must do more than pass a demonstration. It must perform reliably under real production conditions, stay maintainable and deliver measurable value throughout its life.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Amics Automations do?

We design, build, install and support customised automation systems — special purpose machines, end-of-line testers, control panels, PLC/HMI/SCADA software and traceability systems.

Do you handle projects end to end?

Yes. We are turnkey: requirement study, concept, detailed engineering, manufacturing, programming, validation, customer trials, commissioning, training and lifecycle support.

Can you upgrade our existing machines?

Yes. We retrofit older equipment — obsolete PLC/HMI replacement, rewiring, servo/VFD and safety upgrades, and traceability or data-logging additions.

Which communication protocols do you support?

Ethernet TCP/IP, Modbus TCP/RTU, RS-232/485, OPC UA and Ethernet/IP, plus serial device servers and remote I/O.

Have a production challenge?

Tell us what you need to automate or test. We will study the requirement and propose a practical solution.
